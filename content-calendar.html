Process note

How I built this

  • Each card pairs specific platforms with a clear communication job (reach, explain, engage, or invite questions), turning a list of dates into an actionable rollout plan.
  • Calendar moments anchor around the UN Youth Office’s mandate (the General Assembly resolution that created the Office) and flagship initiatives—including the UN Youth Compass, Young Leaders for the SDGs, and Youth Mental Health and Well-being.
  • Visual asset attachments specify deliverable formats (1080×1350 carousels, 9:16 vertical reels, Drupal web cards, and event toolkits) aligned with the role’s dual graphic design and social media scope.
  • The Q4 2026 series card links this board to the social package (Output 03), so the plan and the posts share one story, one look and one source rule.
  • Workflow protocols build in contributor guidance for interns and T-14 day sign-off checkpoints with UNOPS Global Portfolios Office (GPO) and UN system partners.
