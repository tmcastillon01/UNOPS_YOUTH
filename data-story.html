UN Youth Office · Visual Identity & Data Storytelling Sample

Youth, Climate &
Global Decision-Making

Three questions connecting climate risk, youth demographics, and the UN Youth Office mandate: who bears the long-term impact, who sits in the room, and how the UN Youth Compass bridges the gap.

Why visualize the Youth-Climate Nexus? Young people carry the longest exposure to climate and displacement risks, yet institutional policy often stays locked inside dense reports. Each circle below turns global youth and climate data into countable shapes.

1 · Who carries the climate timeline?Over 85% of the world’s 1.2 billion young people live in developing regions facing acute climate exposure.

2 · Who sits in decision-making rooms?People under 30 make up half the global population, yet hold less than 3% of parliamentary seats worldwide.

3 · How does the UN Youth Compass shift participation?Moving youth engagement from one-off consultation to co-designed governance across UN entities.

In one line: 85% of youth live in climate-vulnerable developing regions, under-30s hold just 2.8% of global parliamentary seats, and the UN Youth Compass provides four operational pathways to turn token presence into real institutional voice.

Process note

How I built this sample

  • Design System Continuity: Built on the same design system as the other three samples (Montserrat and DM Sans; navy, amber and green) to demonstrate how complex UN mandates can be made visually approachable for young audiences.
  • Interactive Data Storytelling: Uses HTML5 Canvas to render 280 countable geometric prisms that smoothly morph between three standalone questions and a unified Youth-Climate system.
  • End-to-End Campaign Thinking: Connects high-level data visualization directly to practical deliverables required in the UN Youth Office role—Canva/Adobe templates, event collateral, Drupal publishing, and mobile-first social formats.
