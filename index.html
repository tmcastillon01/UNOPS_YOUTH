🧭 Background

About Me

People trust communication that meets them where they are. That is the approach I bring to design and social media.

During fieldwork in Narok, Kenya, a family I had just met invited me to share their meal. The youngest child had burst into tears at the sight of me; hours later he was waving from the doorway. I think about that whenever I plan communications: people trust content that shows up with care and meets them on their terms.

At UNHCR’s Global Protection Cluster I owned the public Drupal website, designed publications, banners and campaign videos, and grew the Cluster’s organic LinkedIn following by 109.7% in six months. At the WMO I co-led the staff campaign for an intranet migration. At the Local Government Academy I led communications and visual materials for a national webinar series for newly elected local government and youth leaders. I would bring this mix of design, social media and web skills to the UN Youth Office, and I am keen to deepen my work with young audiences, including flagship initiatives such as the UN Youth Compass.
