Output 02 · Orientation deck

30 below 30, to get to 2030

A six-slide briefing for colleagues new to UNOPS youth and climate work. Plain words, no jargon, one idea per slide.

GoalExplain the Youth Engagement Platform and why it matters to delivery, in five minutes.
AudienceUNOPS and UN Youth Office colleagues, new joiners, project teams.
Skills shownInternal comms, information design, plain language.

Process note

How I built this

  • Facts come from UNOPS pages and the UNOPS, UNEP and University of Oxford report Infrastructure for climate action. Every slide names its source.
  • Slide 1 uses UNOPS’s own wording, “half the world is under 30”, so the numbers match the posts in Output 03 and the dots in Output 01.
  • Published facts are separated from the one proposal (slide 5), which is labelled as an independent idea.
  • Colours pass contrast checks on a projector, and no slide relies on red and green together.
  • Before wider circulation, regional youth focal points should check local examples.
