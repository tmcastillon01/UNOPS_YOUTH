Process note

How these samples were built

  • Every number traces to a published page, and each card names its source.
  • Copy follows UNOPS wording: “half the world is under 30”. Claims the sources do not support were left out.
  • Published facts are kept apart from my own proposals, which are labelled.
  • Every image has alt text, and colour is never the only cue.
  • Before anything went live, I would send each claim to the programme team for review.
